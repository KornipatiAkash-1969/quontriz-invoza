Executive Overview
Retail Billing Challenges & The Invoza Solution
❌ Traditional Retail Pain Points
  • Manual typing: Slow, error-prone spreadsheet re-entry of paper receipts.
  • Scattered formats: Bills arrive via WhatsApp photos, scanned chits, and PDFs.
  • Stockout delays: Floor stock shortages lost in verbal or paper notes.
  • Unverified disbursements: Disconnect between goods receiving and payment.
  • Duplicate billing risks: Accidental double payments on identical supplier bills.
✅ The Quontriz Invoza Solution
  • Multi-Engine OCR: Instant text and line-item extraction from PDFs & Images.
  • AI Categorization: Automatic department classification (Grocery, Dairy, etc.).
  • Role Segregation: Dedicated Store Staff and Store Owner portals.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Review: Verify prices, taxes, and duplicates before saving.
  • Inventory Demand Module: Staff logs stock requests; owners toggle & export Excel.

Enterprise Security
Security, Privacy & Zero-Leak Architecture
Cryptographic Defense
  • Salted Bcrypt: Passwords stored as 60-char salted hashes ($2b$10$).
  • Strict Verification: bcrypt.compareSync used exclusively.
  • Zero-Leak API: password_hash omitted from all endpoints.
  • Midnight Expiry: Daily 12:00 AM session reset.
System Safeguards
  • Role Isolation: Staff blocked from Owner portal (403).
  • Admin Protection: Primary developer cannot be deleted.
  • Safe Foreign Keys: Disassociates bills before user deletion.
  • Zero OTP Friction: Direct secure login without SMS bottlenecks.
